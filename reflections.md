Reflection Questions
1. What accessibility enhancements were the most challenging to implement, and why?
ARIA attributes were probably the most challenging because they were completely new to me. I had to learn what the different attributes mean, what information they communicate to assistive technology, and when I should use them.

2. How do ARIA attributes improve the experience for users relying on assistive technologies?
ARIA attributes give assistive technologies information about a page that may not be obvious from the HTML alone. They can tell a user what an element does, whether something is on or off, or announce when something on the page changes.

3. What tools did you use to check color contrast, and how did they help?
I used the WebAIM Contrast Checker to check color contrast. I entered my text and background colors into the checker, and it showed me whether they met WCAG contrast requirements. It made it easy to adjust my colors until they passed.
